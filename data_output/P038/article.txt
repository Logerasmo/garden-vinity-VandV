Effects of drying time on the formation of merged and soft
MAPbI3 grains and their photovoltaic responses

A suitable drying time of the perovskite precursor solution
can facilitate the formation of merged grains while
maintaining the interfacial contact quality between the
perovskite thin ﬁlm and hole transport layer, which highly
increases the open-circuit voltage (VOC) from 1.01 V to 1.15 V
after 30 days and thereby improving the device performance
and lifespan of the resultant inverted perovskite photovoltaic
cells. It is noted that the VOC of the photovoltaic cells is still
higher than 1.15 V after 80 days.                                             See Hsin-Ming Cheng,
