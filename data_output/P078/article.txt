Composite electrode based on single-atom Ni doped graphene for planar
carbon-based perovskite solar cells

h i g h l i g h t s               g r a p h i c a l a b s t r a c t


 Single-atom Ni doped graphene is
 successfully synthesized by
 impregnation method using Ni(NO3)2
 solution.
 A novel composite electrode
 composed of single-atom Ni doped
 graphene (Ni-NG) in the area of
 planar carbon-based perovskite solar
  cells is developed for the ﬁrst time.
 Optimized PSCs with Ni-NG
 composite electrode achieved a
 champion PCE of 12.39%, which
 presents an improvement of 12.74%
 over than that of the PSCs with CE
 composite electrode.
 Efﬁcient hole extraction at the
 perovskite/carbon electrode interface
 can be realized by using abundant
 single-atom Ni active sites, large
 speciﬁc surface area and low work
 function of Ni-NG.
